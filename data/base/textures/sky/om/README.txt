openMERC sky boxes (textures/sky/om, pics/sky/*.txt)

The faces are cut from CC0 HDR panoramas of Poly Haven (https://polyhaven.com, licence CC0 1.0 - public domain):

  train_*.jpg  "Kloppenheim 07 (Pure Sky)" by Greg Zaal (sky edits Jarod Guest), https://polyhaven.com/a/kloppenheim_07_puresky
               (replaces SoF's "train" sky: trn1, ger1, ger2)

Made with a script that cuts the five faces (rt bk lf ft up) from the 8K panorama at 2048 x 2048, exposed to the
full 8-bit range; the "scale" line of the .txt brings the brightness back to the map's.
